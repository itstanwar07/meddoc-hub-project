The process

From submission to publication

A clear, structured pipeline — from your upload to a live published paper.

01

Student uploads paper

A student signs in to their portal and submits a research paper as a PDF, along with the title, abstract, and keywords.

Student portal
02

Researcher submits too

Researchers and professors can also upload their own papers directly, bypassing the student review queue and going straight to admin.

Researcher portal
03

AI scans & categorises

A trained model reads the paper, detects its type — clinical, experimental, review, case study — and assigns it to the right research category.

AI engine
04

Admin reviews & decides

An administrator reviews the paper alongside the AI report, verifies the category and quality, then approves, rejects, or requests revisions.

Admin panel
05

Published & discoverable

Approved papers go live instantly in the open-access repository — searchable and available to the wider academic community.
